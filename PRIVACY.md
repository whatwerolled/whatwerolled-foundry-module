# What We Rolled — Privacy & Data

What We Rolled sends **rolls** and, if you leave it on, the **fights** they happen
in. Of your chat, only messages containing a roll are ever looked at —
in-character banter, GM notes and chat without a roll are ignored.

## What gets sent

For each roll:

- the roll formula, dice, and result;
- the name and avatar of the player who rolled, and which character or token made
  the roll;
- your world name and game system, and their versions;
- whether the roll was a whisper or blind roll, and when it happened.

For each fight, while **Collect encounters** is on:

- the encounter's round and whose turn it is, and the scene's name;
- each participant's name and token picture, its initiative, and whether it is
  hidden or defeated.

## What is never sent

- No passwords or account credentials.
- No chat text that isn't a roll.
- No personal information beyond the player and character names listed above.

## Staying in control

- Nothing is sent until a Campaign ID is set.
- Clear the Campaign ID and all sending stops.
- **Collect rolls** and **Collect encounters** each turn off their own half, in
  **Game Settings → Configure Settings → What We Rolled**.
- Delete a roll's message in Foundry and it drops out of your stats.
